      About

      PDFopener turns PDF and Word (.docx) files into e-books that fit your phone. It pulls out the words, pictures and text colours, finds the chapter headings, and lays everything out in pages you can flip.

      Flip pages: tap the left or right side, swipe, or use the arrow keys.

      Pictures: tap a picture to see it bigger.

      Good to know: scanned PDFs (photos of pages) come through as pictures only, since there's no real text inside. Complex layouts like multi-column magazines may read in a slightly different order.
