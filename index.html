      About

      PDFs stay exactly as they look, but each page is cut into pieces that fit your phone: margins are trimmed so the text is bigger, two-column pages are split into their columns, and cuts are only made in the blank space between lines. Tap a piece to zoom in.

      Word files become a text e-book: pick Reading material or Vocab list when you upload.

      Flip pages: tap the left or right side, swipe, or use the arrow keys.

      Easy layout: long paragraphs are split into short chunks, lists hidden inside sentences become real lists, and notes, tips and definitions get their own boxes. No words are added or changed. Switch to Original in the menu anytime.

      Vocab sheets: choose Vocab list when you upload (or change File type in the menu) and the words become word cards. Choose Hide next to "Vocab meanings" in the menu to test yourself, then tap a card to reveal it.

      Typos: small mistakes (common misspellings, a word typed twice, odd spacing) are fixed and get a faint dotted underline. Hold or hover on one to see what it was.

      Pictures: tap a picture to see it bigger.
